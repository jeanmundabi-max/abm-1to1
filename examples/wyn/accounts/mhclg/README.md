# MHCLG

## Why this organisation is on the list

£17,145,869 a year, and the register is filed under two different names for the same department.

Every figure comes from award notices **the buyer published itself** on Contracts Finder, read
on 8 September 2026. Annual value is the published contract value divided by its published
term, and the page says so wherever it does that arithmetic.

A contract that is about to expire is the only moment its price is negotiable. That expiry
date is what put this organisation on the list, and it is a statutory publication duty rather
than anything inferred.

## On the committee table

The page names the seats that have to agree and what each asks first. **It does not name the
individuals.** Where a gov.uk organogram publishes the person, that name is deliberately not
reproduced here; where it does not, the seat was left unnamed rather than inferred.
