# BRIEF — WYN × HM Revenue & Customs

**Self-authored, not interviewed.** WYN is not in the room: this is a layer 2 demonstration
and the operator infers the brief from what WYN publishes. Marked so the next reader knows.

---

## The eight answers

**1. Vibe, three to five words, and three references from any medium.**
Audited. Quiet. Unarguable. References: a Companies House filing, the Shipping Forecast read
aloud, and the ruled margin of an accounts ledger. Not a fintech site.

**2. The scroll journey, in order.**
The register they published. The one contract that comes up first. Why a renewal is the only
moment the number moves. What the same suppliers charge elsewhere. What the document is. What
it is not. Who has to sign what. The ask.

**3. The energy curve.**
Flat and cold at the open, because the reader is a civil servant being sold to and any warmth
reads as a pitch. One rise at the register drawing itself. Quiet again through the mechanism.
The peak is the two rails halting on the same date. Calm to the close.

**4. How it should feel, stage by stage, and the ONE moment they remember.**
Recognition, then a small unease, then relief that nothing is being asked of them. **The moment:
two figures set in identical weight, halting on the same date, and the film never says which is
larger.** They do the arithmetic themselves, and a conclusion they reach cannot be argued with.

**5. One thing this page does that no page they have seen does.**
It cites them to themselves and then refuses to draw the conclusion. Every other vendor page
tells a department it is overpaying. This one says where the number sits and stops.

**6. How far from premium minimal.**
Editorial, close to minimal. This audience is not moved by a gradient. Type, rules, and one
accent.

**7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes.**
One unbroken world. The whole page is one ruled register, and the film is a travel along it.
Cuts are where a thing gets hidden, and this reader is trained to notice.

**8. Assets already owned.**
The register itself: 1,909 award notices in `00-inputs/contracts_live.csv`, sixteen of them
HMRC's. WYN's wordmark. No footage, no photography, and none is wanted.

## The peak

**"Two numbers, the same weight, the same date, and nobody told me which was worse."**
Act 4. It gets the largest span and the only accent.

## The tell-someone sentence

It's the page where **they showed me my own contracts and then refused to tell me the answer.**

## Authored silence

Act 3 has no figure and no accent. It is one sentence on white. That is deliberate, so the
verification pass does not read it as dead scroll.
