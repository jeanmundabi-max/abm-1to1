# BRIEF, ElevenAgents × BT (rebuild)

**Self-authored, not interviewed.** Layer 2 demonstration; ElevenLabs is not in the room. Rebuilt 2026-09-11 to `sops/07b` and `sops/08b` after the dry run was reviewed and deleted.

## The eight answers

**1. Vibe, three to five words, and three references.** Instrument, not brochure. References: the contact-centre wallboard in a real contact centre, a dark operations dashboard, the Revolut cards (Out of Tolerance, Wall of Screens).

**2. The scroll journey, in order.** The board they clicked, alive. Their tiles, from their file. Why the first tile sits where it sits. They hold, and let go. The fix, named, as what changes on their board. People who run a board like theirs, in their words. What arrives and what does not. Two ways in. Who decides. The board with the new tile lit, above one field.

**3. The energy curve.** Loud at the open, because it is the ad continuing. Quiet through the why. The peak is the hold. Loud again when the new tile lights at the ask.

**4. How it should feel, and the ONE moment.** Recognised (that is my board), uneasy (that is my number), then the moment: **they pressed the wait tile, let go at seven seconds, and the board lit a tile that was not there before.**

**5. One thing this page does that no page they have seen does.** It is their wallboard, with one tile they do not have yet.

**6. How far from premium minimal.** Not minimal. Dense and instrument-like: a dark board on ElevenLabs' off-white ground, the ElevenAgents blue-green on one tile only. The dry run answered "quiet, monochrome" here and came out pale; this is the corrected answer.

**7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes.** One world: the board. It is the hero, the strip at the top, the peak and the close.

**8. Assets already owned.** The file: Ofcom's Comparing customer service report. ElevenLabs' wordmark. No footage, none wanted.
