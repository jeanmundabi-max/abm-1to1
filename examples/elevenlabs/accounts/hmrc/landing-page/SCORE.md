# SCORE, ElevenAgents × HMRC (rebuild)

## Page grammar: THE WALLBOARD

Chosen over split stage (Hays), ruled ledger (HMRC), the transcript (the deleted dry run), printed chapters, live surface, worldflight, brutalist wall and gallery. The page is the object from the buyer's own day, the hold message, with HMRC's own figures on it: a dark instrument carrying only published tiles, with one tile in the client's colour that does not exist yet. Per `sops/08b`, the hook is the object, not a sentence.

- **Nav:** none. A fixed strip of the board along the top carries three of their tiles and the dark Answered tile, which lights when the reader reaches the ask.
- **Hero:** the board alive (the film) beside the promise.
- **Close:** the board again, the new tile lit, above the form.

## Signature move: THE HOLD

Inside the board. The reader presses and holds; the wait counts up at the true rate of 11 minutes 35 seconds. On release the board says where they stopped and lights the Answered tile. Never says the number is bad. Pointer handler plus --sc-p on the peak act.

## The film: THE BOARD, LIT

32 seconds, four beats overlapped. The dark board. Beat 1: the first tile counts up to 11:35. Beat 2: the other published tiles arrive. Beat 3: the same tile on a peer's board (Deutsche Telekom's line, quoted). Beat 4: the Answered tile lights in the ElevenAgents colour, 0:01, the only accent in the film. Source at 20% ink on every frame. No countdown. Motion QA at 0 repeats.
