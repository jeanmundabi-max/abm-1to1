# BRIEF: Chewy, for Serval

**Reader.** The person at Chewy who owns the internal ticket queue. Not the CTO. The manager
who watches the queue climb every time a hiring wave lands.

**The angle that is only Chewy's.** Size is not the story, shape is. A workforce with a large
part-time and fulfilment population turns over, and every turn is two IT jobs, one at each end.
That is a different argument from Wayfair's, which is about a team that got cut.

**How far from premium minimal.** Not minimal. Dense and instrument-like. This reader is being
asked to believe a machine can close their tickets, and a pale page reads as a claim with
nothing behind it.

**The world.** Chewy's own e-commerce blue is not used. Serval's violet #703fdc marks one thing
only: the share of the queue that stops needing a person.

**The signature move.** Press and hold to run both probes live, with the nonsense control last.

**The number they supply.** Requests per employee per month. We do not have it and will not
invent it. 18,000 is filed; the rate is theirs.

**What is inferred.** The offer. Serval publishes no pricing and no trial terms. Disclosed in the
footer, never on the button.
