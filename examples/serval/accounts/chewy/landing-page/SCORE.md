# SCORE: Chewy

**Grammar.** Instrument, not document. Opens on a live probe readout rather than a claim, and
the reader can re-run it by hand.

**The feeling curve.** Recognition, then proof it is really about them via the nonsense control,
then the weight of 18,000 against 580, then understanding that a ticket system routes and never
resolves, then agency at the dial, then one line of respite, then peers in the same seat, then a
yes that costs ten lines of typing.

**The signature move.** Press and hold for five seconds. Both service desk probes run in sequence
with the control at the end. `pointer-events:auto` is forced on the wrap and a transparent rect
covers the whole SVG, so the press lands anywhere on the ring.

**The advertiser's colour.** Serval's violet marks the share of the queue that would stop needing a
person. Nothing else on the page uses it.

**The hero film. Not built in this pass, and that is a decision, not an omission.** The first
viewport carries a live instrument, which is the stronger opening for a reader whose first
question is whether this is really about them. The film belongs at the top of the probe section
and is the next pass.

**Verified, and how.** Driven in real Chrome via Playwright at 390x844 and 1440x900, pointer held
5.3 seconds, ring reaching stroke-dashoffset 0 on both, measured after the scroll settles rather
than before, because measuring first and pressing later produces a false negative on the taller
viewport.
