workforce that will abandon any internal tool that costs more than it returns.

**The angle that is only Riot's.** There is no filing. Riot is private and Tencent-owned, and
the three data vendors that sell its headcount disagree by a thousand people. So this page makes
its refusal the argument: it names the vendors' disagreement, declines to use any of them, and
runs entirely on things Riot announced and things Riot's own servers answered.

**Why that is stronger, not weaker.** A reader at a private company is used to being sold
estimates about themselves. Being told which number is not trustworthy, and why, does more for
credibility than any figure would.

**The world.** Serval's violet marks the share of the queue that stops needing a person.
