is the sharper instrument for this reader.

**The feeling curve.** Recognition through a number that is too small to be a headline, proof via
the CNAME and the nonsense control, then the trap being named (flat headcount removes the hiring

`pointer-events:auto` forced on the wrap, transparent rect over the whole SVG.

person.

**The hero film. Not built in this pass, and that is a decision, not an omission.** The film is
the next pass; the live instrument opens better for a reader whose first question is whether the
page is really about them.

seconds, ring reaching 0 on both, measured after the scroll settles.
