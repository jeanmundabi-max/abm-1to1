# SCORE: DoorDash

**Grammar.** Instrument, not document. This is the build where the instrument shows a negative
result as well as a positive one, and the negative is load-bearing.

**The feeling curve.** Recognition through a dated event rather than a headcount, proof via the
printed miss and the control, then the weight of per-person integration work, then understanding,
agency at the dial with an explicit invitation to model the integration months, respite, peers,
and a cheap yes.

**The signature move.** Press and hold five seconds. Three requests in sequence, the middle one
returning nothing, the control last.

**The client's colour.** Serval's violet marks only the share of the queue that stops needing a
person.

**The hero film. Not built in this pass, and that is a decision, not an omission.** The film is
the next pass.

**Verified, and how.** Real Chrome via Playwright at 390x844 and 1440x900, pointer held 5.3
seconds, ring reaching 0 on both, measured after the scroll settles.
