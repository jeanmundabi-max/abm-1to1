# The ad: DoorDash

![The ad card for DoorDash](card.png)

**You cannot open the real ad.** A LinkedIn ad preview is only visible to someone signed in
with access to the advertising account it lives in, so the link would be useless here.
Everything the ad carries is written out below instead, exactly as it was configured.

## What it says

**Body copy**

> A customer can watch their order move through four states in real time. An employee asking for access gets assigned to a person, and then nothing until somebody has a free afternoon.

**Headline**

> You track a burrito better than an access request.

## How it was built

**Headline formula: Conversation.** One of six, chosen before the line was written rather
than picked afterwards to describe what came out.

**The card is an object from DoorDash's own world**, with Serval's violet marking the one element
that carries the meaning, and DoorDash's own filed number as the biggest thing on it. The test is
to cover the words: the picture alone should say who it is for.

Ad set `Serval - US - Stage1Cold - Unaware - SingleImage - DoorDash`, id `AD_SET_ID`, status
**DRAFT**. Nothing was activated and nothing was spent.
