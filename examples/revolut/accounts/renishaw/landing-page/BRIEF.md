# BRIEF: Renishaw plc, for Revolut Business

> **Provenance: derived from the working session with the operator, not a fresh interview.**
> Jean gave this direction across a long build conversation on 2 and 3 September 2026,
> including three rejections of earlier work. His words are quoted where he said them. This is
> not self-authored and it is not a fresh eight-question pass.

## The eight answers

**1. Vibe, and three references from any medium.**
Instrument, not brochure. The references are not sites, they are objects: **a metrology
readout**, **a shop wall of screens**, **a placement board**. All three came out of the
creative council work Jean approved, and he called the resulting cards *"magnifiques"*.

**2. The scroll journey, in his words.**
The page is *"une extension de la single image"* the reader just clicked, made *"interactive
mais toujours dans le même but"*. So: the ad card arrives alive, the company's own filed
sentence lands, the gap opens, the proof from a real enterprise customer, then the ask.

**3. The energy curve.**
Jean rejected flat three separate times, twice in the same word: *"flat"*, and *"c'est nul"*.
Loud at the open because it is the ad continuing, quiet in the middle where the filing speaks,
and the loudest moment is the gap, not the ask.

**4. How it should feel, and the ONE moment.**
Recognised, then uneasy, then curious, then settled. **The one moment: the reader tries to
read the gap and cannot.** The page shows them the two rates and refuses to give them the
difference, because the difference is what the report produces. They should think: *they
showed me the space where my number goes and would not fill it in.*

**5. One thing no site they have seen does.**
Withhold the number it is offering to produce, and let the visitor try to measure it by hand.

**6. Distance from premium-minimal.**
Not minimal. Three rejections were all of restraint that read as emptiness. Dense and
instrument-like, closer to editorial than to premium-minimal.

**7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?**
**Distinct scenes, but one instrument.** Each account gets its own world and the world never
leaves the frame.

**8. Assets already owned.**
Three 32s silent films we rendered, three 4:5 ad cards, and Revolut's own Lyca Mobile customer
film. Jean's constraint: the proof video *must not* share a viewport with the hero film.

## The feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | What on screen causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Recognition | *that is my ad, and it is moving* | the card the reader clicked, animating, in the left column |
| 2 Exposure | uneasy | their own filed sentence, quoted whole, with the date |
| 3 **The gap** | **frustrated, then curious** | **the two rates open apart and the space between them stays empty** |
| 4 Settled | relieved | an enterprise customer, on film, in Revolut's own words |
| 5 Commitment | decided | the split collapses and the form takes the full width |

## The peak

Act 3, and it gets the most scroll room on the page.

**The sentence a visitor says to a friend:** *"They put my rate and the interbank rate side by
side, left a gap between them, and would not tell me how big it was."*

## The tell-someone sentence

**It's the site where the gap between two rates is the whole page, and it never fills it in.**

## Authored silence

Act 2 holds with almost nothing moving except the divider. That is deliberate, it is the quiet
before the peak, and the verification pass should not read it as dead scroll.

## Addendum, 2026-09-08: what in this brief is Renishaw plc's and what is the campaign's

The eight answers above came from the operator's direction across the whole Revolut build and
they govern all three accounts. They are not a fresh interview for this one and the file has
always said so.

What is this account's alone is the finding, the world drawn from it, and the paragraph that
inverts. Hays did not hedge, and the Hays page says in as many words that the hedging story is not theirs. Renishaw hedge across three currencies and say so, and their own sentence ranks forward contract income above the rate as the cause. So the third paragraph of act 2b says the opposite on this page.

The feeling curve and the peak are unchanged, because the shape of the argument is unchanged:
the reader arrives on their own filed sentence, the gap opens, they perform the measurement they
cannot resolve, and the page asks for one file.
