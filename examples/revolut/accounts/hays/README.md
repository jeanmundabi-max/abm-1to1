# Hays plc

**Recruitment.** Every figure on the page comes from Hays plc's own preliminary report for FY26, year ended 30 June 2026.

## What makes this page different from the other runs

It **refuses to compute the number**.

> "Hays' conversion volume is not public and neither is the spread you are paying, so no
> honest page can tell you the figure. What it can do is show you the shape of it. Move these
> and the arithmetic is yours."

The page carries two sliders. The reader supplies the volume and the spread, and the page does
the arithmetic in front of them. Nothing is asserted about Hays plc, because the two inputs a
real figure would need are not public. This is the strongest answer in the repository to the
problem of putting a number on a cold page, and it is worth comparing against the Serval pages,
which solve the same problem a different way.
